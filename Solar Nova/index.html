FAQ

Answers to the questions we hear most often.

How long does a solar installation take?

Most residential systems are installed within one to three weeks after design approval, while larger commercial projects may take longer based on site conditions and permitting.

What is the average cost of a solar system?

Costs vary based on system size, project complexity, battery storage needs, and available incentives. We provide transparent estimates tailored to your property and goals.

Do you offer maintenance and support?

Yes. SolarNova provides ongoing system monitoring, maintenance plans, troubleshooting, and performance optimization to keep every installation working efficiently.

How long do solar panels last?

Most modern solar panels are designed to operate efficiently for 25 to 30 years or longer, with output gradually declining over time.

How much can I save on my energy bills?

Energy savings depend on usage patterns, system size, and local utility rates. Many SolarNova clients see meaningful reductions in monthly electricity costs after installation.
